Download Store POS for Windows

The full register for your store's PC: scanning, receipts, cash drawer, inventory and employees. Your store's data stays on your own computer.

Enter your owner code

Type the owner code you received (it starts with SPOS-). The same code activates Store POS after you install it.

Needs Windows 10 or 11.

After you download

  1. Open StorePOS-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Store POS installs and opens. On the setup screen enter your store details, your owner PIN, and paste the same owner code into License key.
  4. Want tablets, or to manage the store from home? In Store POS go to Settings → Tablets and choose Link to my Trivedi account, then sign in at trivedipos.com/pos.
