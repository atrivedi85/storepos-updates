PdfStudio Privacy Policy

For the PdfStudio apps: the Android app and the web version at trivedipos.com/pdfstudio. Effective November 24, 2026.

PdfStudio is a PDF editor and scanner published by Indo American LLC ("we", "us") under the Trivedi POS brand. This page explains, in plain words, what information PdfStudio handles, why, where it is kept, who can see it, and how you can have it deleted. (Our separate Store POS app has its own privacy policy.)

Short version. Your PDFs stay on your phone unless you choose to save them to your cloud files or send them for signature. We do not run ads, we do not sell your information, and we have no analytics or tracking tools in the app. You need a Trivedi POS account to sign in, and you can ask us to delete it at any time (how to delete your account).

1. Your account

PdfStudio uses the same Trivedi POS / Ledger Desk account as our other products. Accounts are created by invitation from the Trivedi POS owner; there is no public sign-up. To get one, email atrivedi2508@gmail.com.

  • What we keep: your username, your display name, your email address if one is set on the account (used for optional two-step sign-in codes), and when the account was created and last used.
  • Password: your password is turned into a sign-in value inside your browser or app; the password itself is not sent to us. We store only a salted, one-way hash (scrypt) of that value, never a readable password.
  • Sign-in sessions: a sign-in lasts 30 days. We store a one-way hash of the session token, its expiry, and the kind of device and browser (for example "Chrome on Android") and the IP address it signed in from, so you can be signed out if the account is turned off.
  • Failed sign-ins: to block password guessing we keep the username and IP address of failed attempts for about 15 minutes.

Why: to let you sign in and to keep your account secure (account management).

2. Your PDFs and cloud files

  • On your device: files you open from your phone are processed on your phone. The app keeps a "Recent files" list (up to 20 documents, including a copy of each file and a small preview image) inside the app's own storage on your device so they open again without internet. Scans you make with the camera stay on your device until you save them to your phone, share them, or put them in your cloud files. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes this local copy.
  • In the cloud (only if you use it): if you save a PDF to Cloud / "My files", we store the PDF and its name, size and dates in your private storage on our server. Only your account can open it. Limits are 50 MB per file, 500 files and 500 MB in total.

Why: to open, edit and save your documents and let you reach them from your phone, the web and Windows (app functionality). We do not look at the contents of your files and do not use them for advertising or to train any AI.

3. Saved signatures, contacts and profile

  • Saved signatures and initials (pictures you draw, type or upload) are stored with your account so they follow you to every device. A copy is also kept on the device you use.
  • Saved contacts (the name and email of people you have sent documents to) are stored with your account, so the "Send for signature" list is the same everywhere.
  • Sender profile (the name signers see, and the email address that should be told when everyone has signed).

You can remove any of these inside the app at any time.

4. Sending a document for signature

If you use Sign → Send for others to sign, we store the following so the signers can complete it and you can see the result:

  • the PDF you send, its title and file name, your optional message, and where the signing boxes are;
  • each signer's name and email address and their private signing link;
  • what each signer enters: their signature or initials picture, typed text, the date, and their time zone;
  • a record of what happened and when (sent, opened, signed, declined, reminders), together with the IP address and browser/device description of each signer when they open, sign or decline. This is the audit trail that appears on the certificate page added to the finished PDF;
  • the finished signed PDF with its certificate page.

Signers do not need an account. Anyone who has a signer's private link can open that signer's document, so signers should not forward it. A signer must tick a box agreeing to sign electronically before submitting.

Email: PdfStudio is built to email signing links and completion notices through an email service provider (Resend). Email sending is currently switched off, so you share the signing link yourself (for example by text or your own email), and we do not send email on your behalf. If we switch it on, signers' names and email addresses will be passed to that provider only to deliver those messages.

Why: to let people sign documents and to give both sides a trustworthy record (app functionality).

5. Camera

The Android app asks for the Camera permission only when you use the scanner. It uses the camera for video only (never the microphone) to take photos of paper pages. Edge detection, cropping and the black-and-white and other filters run on your device. The picture leaves your device only if you later save the PDF to your cloud files, share it, or send it for signature. You can turn the permission off in your phone's settings; you can still open PDFs and photos from your files.

6. Files and photos on your phone

PdfStudio opens files you pick, share to it, or open with it. It does not scan your phone for other files. When you save, Android's own "Save as" screen lets you choose where the file goes.

7. Who we share information with

  • The people you send documents to. Signers see the document you send, your name and message, and their own boxes. Everyone who signed can download the finished copy, which shows the names and signing times on its certificate page.
  • Service providers that run PdfStudio for us: Cloudflare hosts the website, sign-in and storage (Workers, R2 storage, D1 and Durable Object databases) and sees ordinary connection data such as IP addresses. GitHub hosts the app download file. They handle data only to provide those services.
  • If the law requires it, we may disclose information to the proper authorities.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and PdfStudio has no ads or third-party analytics.

8. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. The Android app does not allow unencrypted connections.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form (see section 1). Session tokens and signing links are long random values; session tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • Cloud files are kept in private storage that is not publicly reachable, and every request is checked against your account. Cloudflare encrypts stored data at rest.

No system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong password and sign out on shared devices.

9. How long we keep information

  • Cloud files, saved signatures, contacts and profile: until you delete them or your account is deleted.
  • Sign-in sessions: up to 30 days, or until you sign out.
  • Documents sent for signature and their audit trail are kept until you ask us to delete them (they are not removed automatically), because signers may need the signed copy later.
  • Data on your device: until you remove it or uninstall the app.

10. Deleting your data and account

You can delete cloud files, saved signatures and contacts yourself inside the app. To delete your whole account and its data, follow the steps on our Delete account page. We complete requests within 30 days.

11. Children

PdfStudio is a business and productivity tool for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and accounts are created by invitation only. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how PdfStudio handles information, we will update this page and its date.
