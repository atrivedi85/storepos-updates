Delete your account

How to delete a PdfStudio or Trivedi POS account and the data stored with it, or just delete some of your data and keep the account.

Option 1. Delete my account and data

Applies to PdfStudio (Android app, web and Windows) and the Trivedi POS account used to sign in to it.

  1. Send an email from the email address on your account to atrivedi2508@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Delete my account, and include your username.
  3. We reply to confirm it is you. If your email is not on the account, we may ask you to confirm another way.
  4. We delete your account and data within 30 days of confirming, and email you when it is done.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account: username, name, email, password hash and sign-in sessions.
  • Your cloud files (PDFs saved in Cloud / My files).
  • Your saved signatures and initials.
  • Your saved contacts and sender profile.
  • Documents you sent for signature, with their signing links, signers' entries and the audit trail we hold.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Copies other people already have. A signer who received or downloaded a signed copy keeps it; we cannot take it back from their device or email.
  • Server logs and backups held by our hosting provider (Cloudflare), which expire on their normal schedule, normally within 30 days.
  • Records we must keep by law or need to resolve a dispute. We will tell you if this applies.

Your Trivedi POS account may also be used for Store POS back office, Ledger Desk or Hotel Desk. Deleting the account removes it from all of them; tell us in the email if you only want to leave PdfStudio.

Option 2. Delete some data but keep my account

You can do this yourself, right now, in PdfStudio:

  • Cloud files: open your cloud files (My files) and press Delete next to the file.
  • Saved signatures: Sign → Add signature → Saved → the red × on a signature.
  • Saved contacts: Sign → Send for others to sign, then press the × next to a person in your saved contacts.
  • Data on this phone: Home → the ⋮ menu on a recent file → Remove from list, or clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → PdfStudio, or uninstall the app.

Want something removed that you cannot remove yourself (for example a document you sent for signature)? Email atrivedi2508@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data.
