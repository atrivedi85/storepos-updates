PdfStudio users

How accounts work

PdfStudio uses your Ledger Desk accounts. Everyone with a Ledger Desk login can sign in to PdfStudio with the same username and password, and appears below after their first sign-in. To add someone, invite them in Ledger Desk. To reset a password or turn someone off completely, do it in Ledger Desk.

People who have used PdfStudio

"Disable" only blocks PdfStudio for that person. "Remove files" deletes their cloud PDFs and their PdfStudio record (they come back if they sign in again).
