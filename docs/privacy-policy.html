Store POS - Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Store POS is point-of-sale software for retail stores, published by Indo American LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when a store uses Store POS on Android tablets or Windows computers.

Information stays on the store's devices

Store POS stores the store's data - items, prices, stock, sales and refunds, employee names, PINs, work hours and pay rates, vendor details, and the last four digits of payment cards when a cashier enters them - only on the store's own tablet(s) and computer. We do not receive, collect, sell or share this information. Store POS does not include advertising or analytics.

Payment cards

Store POS does not read, process or store full card numbers. Cards are processed on the store's separate card terminal. A cashier may type the last four digits so a receipt can be found later.

Network connections

  • Updates: the app checks for and downloads program updates from GitHub (github.com) over HTTPS. GitHub may log the device's IP address as part of serving the download (see GitHub's privacy statement).
  • Store network: if the store links a tablet to its own Store POS computer, the two exchange store data directly over the store's local network. This data does not pass through us.
  • Printers: receipts are sent to the store's own network or Bluetooth printer.

Permissions

Bluetooth is used only to connect to the store's receipt printer. Internet access is used for updates and to reach the store's own computer and printers.

Data retention and deletion

The store controls its data. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on that tablet. Backups the store makes are saved where the store chooses.

Children

Store POS is business software for adults and is not directed to children.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
