STORE POS 1.3 — SETUP
=====================

1. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All..." (don't run it from inside the zip).
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Install Store POS".
   - If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway".
     (This appears because the app isn't code-signed yet.)
3. Store POS opens full screen. The first time, it loads your 5,504 items from Exatouch,
   then imports your sales history (191,854 orders, May 2021 - Oct 22 2026) in the background.
4. Sign in with PIN 1234 (Owner). Cashier PIN is 1111. Change both in Settings > Users & PINs.

USERS & PERMISSIONS (Settings > Users & PINs)
Pick a role (Owner, Manager, Cashier), then tap the Permissions button to tick exactly what
each person may do: discounts, price changes, returns, refunds, pay outs, open drawer,
see items (read only), edit items, add/adjust inventory, delete items, promos, see reports,
print/PDF reports, settings. If a cashier tries something they can't do, a manager types
their PIN to approve it - the approval is saved on the transaction.

ALREADY INSTALLED AN EARLIER VERSION?
Just run "Install Store POS" again. Everything you have is kept.
Your data lives in %APPDATA%\Store POS - installing never touches it.

FUTURE UPDATES (no more big downloads)
From 1.3 on, updates come as a small file named like StorePOS-Update-1.3.1.posupdate.
Download it on the POS computer and double-click it (or Settings > Import / Backup >
Install update file). Store POS makes a backup, installs it and restarts. Your data is kept.
If an update ever fails to start, Store POS automatically goes back to the version that worked.

PRINTERS (Settings > Printers & Drawer)
The list shows every printer on this PC: tap Receipts / Reports / Labels to choose what each
one prints, Test to check it, and "Add a printer" to open Windows printer setup.

RECEIPT PRINTER CONNECTION
Pick how it's connected, then tap "Test print":
  - USB printer with its Windows driver installed (Epson TM series, Star TSP/SP, Bixolon,
    Citizen, Xprinter, Rongta, Munbyn, HPRT, SNBC, Sewoo, POS-80/POS-58 and others):
      "Windows printer - use its driver"
  - If receipts come out blank, tiny or slow, or the printer uses the "Generic / Text Only"
    driver: "Windows printer - send receipt commands directly"
  - Network / Wi-Fi / Ethernet printer: enter its IP address (port 9100).
    Hold FEED while turning the printer on to print a self-test page with its IP.
  - Serial or USB-COM printer: pick the COM port and speed (usually 9600 or 19200).
  For the direct options, choose the printer language: ESC/POS (Epson and almost all brands)
  or Star Line Mode (older Star printers). Characters per line: 48 for most 80 mm printers,
  42 for Epson TM-T88, 32 for 58 mm.

CASH DRAWER
Plug the drawer into the receipt printer's DK/drawer port, then tap "Test drawer".
If it doesn't open, try "Pin 5", or set the drawer to open in the printer driver
and choose "Printer driver opens it when anything prints".

BARCODE SCANNER
Plug in any USB scanner and scan: it just works (keyboard-style scanners, wired,
wireless or Bluetooth). Codes ending in Enter, Tab or nothing all work, and the POS
also matches codes missing the check digit, leading zeros, or short UPC-E codes.
If your scanner is set to "USB COM / virtual serial" mode, pick its COM port in Settings.
Use the "Scan test" box in Settings to check any barcode.

PAYMENTS
- Split payment: tap "Split", enter the cash part, then pick Credit/Debit for the rest.
- Card payments ask for the last 4 digits of the card (turn off in Store & Receipt).

TRANSACTIONS SCREEN
- Scan an item to see every sale of it and when it last sold.
- Type 4 digits to find transactions by the card's last 4 (works for old Exatouch sales too).

ITEMS & BARCODE LABELS
- New item without a barcode? Tap "New barcode" next to SKU, Save, then "Print Labels".
- Choose the label printer and label size in Settings > Printers & Drawer.

REPORTS
Reports screen buttons:  CSV = spreadsheet of item sales,  PDF = download the report as a
PDF file (saved to your Downloads folder),  Receipt = short summary on the receipt printer,
Print = full-page report on any printer connected to this PC.

LOGO & ON-SCREEN KEYBOARD
- Upload your logo in Settings > Store & Receipt. It prints on receipts and reports.
- A touch keyboard pops up when typing. Turn it off in Store & Receipt if you use a real keyboard.

GOOD TO KNOW
- To leave the POS: Settings > Scanner, App & Backups > Exit Store POS (owner only).
- Your data is saved in:  %APPDATA%\Store POS\data\pos.db
  Daily backups go to:    %APPDATA%\Store POS\backups  (last 14 kept)
- Uninstall from Start menu > Store POS > Uninstall Store POS (keeps your data).
